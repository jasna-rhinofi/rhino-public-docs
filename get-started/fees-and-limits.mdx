## How Rhino.fi fees work

Rhino.fi offers a pricing structure which has the flexibility to suit clients throughout their growth journey and provides full access to the Rhino.fi Stablecoin Activation Stack, with a number of optional extensions to supercharge your Rhino.fi use. 

For more information, visit [rhino.fi/pricing](https://rhino.fi/pricing).

## Subscription

The subscription tiers are designed to grow with your company as it scales.

- The **Starter** tier is ideal for early teams who are in beta testing and at low volumes.
- The **Growth** tier allows for predictable spend at scale for companies growing their offering.
- The **Scale** tier is a match for scaling teams with stablecoins at the core of their offering.
- The **Enterprise** tier is for companies where stablecoins are a critical flow, with tailored economics and coverage for the highest volumes and values.

Each tier has a fixed monthly cost, with the ability to continue into overages for an uninterrupted service.

Clients have access to the Rhino.fi Stablecoin Activation Stack, including Smart Deposit Addresses, Console access, enterprise-grade transaction volume and value support, embedded KYT and AML, multi-chain support across 30+ chains and more.

You can also enable a wide variety of extensions, including:

- **Tron, Solana and Bitcoin Access**, which provides access to these chains for bridging and Smart Deposit Addresses.
- **Advanced Fee & Limit Management**, which provides comprehensive control over fees and visibility of limits.
- **1:1 Stablecoin Settlement**, which enables zero-slippage USDC/USDT conversions.
- **Enhanced Compliance & Risk Management**, which adds alerting and custom configuration on top of Rhino.fi's robust setup.

See [Extensions](/get-started/extensions) for the full list.

## Trial accounts

Trial accounts are for testing before you subscribe. Each transaction pays a fee, deducted from the amount sent on the destination chain. Trial fees are for testing only and are not a guide to subscription pricing. See [Trial accounts: what's included and how it differs from a subscription](https://learn.rhino.fi/articles/8966431786-trial-accounts-what-s-included-and-how-it-differs-from-a-subscription).

## Onchain costs

Rhino.fi covers the onchain costs of moving funds from Smart Deposit Addresses and of settlement through the Rhino.fi Bridge, and you pay your fixed monthly fee. This keeps your costs predictable and removes the volatility of onchain costs.

The exception is Tron, Solana and Bitcoin, which have additional onchain costs. For each of these chains, you choose who pays: you (invoiced at month end) or your end customer (deducted from the amount they receive). See [Tron, Solana and Bitcoin Access](/get-started/extensions/tron-access).

## Fees in quotes

Fee information is still shown in every quote. For bridge and bridge+swap, request a quote with `POST /bridge/quote/bridge-swap/user`. For Smart Deposit Addresses, you can get the fees before the deposit by adding `isSda: "true"` to the same request (see [Pricing an SDA deposit](/api-integration/bridge#pricing-an-sda-deposit)). Rhino.fi honours the quoted fee until the quote expires, so there are no surprises at settlement. The expiry time is in the response's `expiresAt` field.

Some fields in the quote's `fees` object apply only to clients on [legacy pricing](https://learn.rhino.fi/articles/4088519114-how-fees-are-applied). On a subscription, most fees are covered by your plan.

## Client surcharge

You can add an extra charge that is passed directly through to your customers for their Rhino.fi activity. This is called a client surcharge. It can be a fixed dollar amount, a fixed percentage, or both. The total collected is deducted from your end-of-month invoice.

For example, Client B charges its customers \$1 for any Rhino.fi activity. At the end of the month, the total client surcharge Rhino.fi collected on behalf of Client B is \$1,000. Client B's end-of-month invoice is its fixed subscription cost minus \$1,000.

To set the client surcharge for specific customers or routes, or to change it once set, enable the [Advanced Fee & Limit Management](/get-started/extensions/advanced-fee-management) extension.

To discuss which approach suits your company, contact your Rhino.fi account manager.
