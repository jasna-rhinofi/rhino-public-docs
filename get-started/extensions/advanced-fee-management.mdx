**Extension tier:** Free + Premium

The Advanced Fee & Limit Management extension gives you control over how fees are presented and collected in your integration. It's designed for clients who want to remove fee friction for their end users entirely, or to use their Rhino.fi integration as a revenue source.

You use it in the Rhino.fi Console, where you can view your limits and set fee policies.

## Client surcharge

Subscription clients can add an extra charge that is passed directly through to their customers for their Rhino.fi activity. This is called a client surcharge. It can be a fixed dollar amount, a fixed percentage, or both. The total collected is deducted from your end-of-month invoice. See [Fees](/get-started/fees-and-limits#client-surcharge).

For example:

- Client A adds a client surcharge of 1%.
- Client B adds a client surcharge of \$0.50.
- Client C adds a client surcharge of 0.25% + \$0.30.

Without this extension, the surcharge applies to all your customers and all routes.

### Fee policies

With this extension, you can set a **default policy** for one or more projects, which applies to all customers and all routes. You can then add **fee policies** that override the default when their conditions are met. Fee policies can be set at the customer level or the route level.

- **Customer fee policies.** Apply a surcharge to one or more specific customers, identified by a customer ID or by their Rhino.fi Smart Deposit Addresses. This lets you charge different rates to different groups, for example 0.1% for VIP customers and 0.4% for everyone else.
- **Route fee policies.** Apply a surcharge to one or more chains, as the source chain, the destination chain or both. For example, a \$1 fee from Solana to Base, and a \$2 fee for any transaction involving Ethereum, as source or destination. You can also set rates for specific tokens, as the source or destination token, or combine chains and tokens in one policy.

Together, these let you set fee policies that match your business.

We're adding more fee policy options. If you need a structure that isn't covered above, contact your account manager.

## Limit information

The extension also shows your Smart Deposit Address limits and, where the extension is enabled, your 1:1 Stablecoin Settlement limits, directly in the Console.

- **SDA limits** show how many Smart Deposit Addresses you've created, in total and over different time periods, and how that compares with your total SDA limit. You can also see the minimum and maximum deposit per SDA, for each project.
- **1:1 Stablecoin Settlement limits** show which projects have 1:1 enabled, your 30-day volume against the limit (including daily volume, the rolling 30-day total and projected volume) and the maximum USD per recipient.

For more on how limits work, see [Limits](/get-started/limits).
