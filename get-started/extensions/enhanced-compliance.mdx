**Extension tier:** Free + Premium

## Baseline compliance (all accounts)

Every Rhino.fi account includes KYT/AML screening. Every deposit is screened before it is credited, and any deposit that fails compliance checks is blocked, so you never receive funds that fail Rhino.fi's standard checks. Every account includes:

- KYT screening on 100% of inbound deposits to Smart Deposit Addresses
- Automatic blocking of deposits from flagged addresses
- Compliance event logging, available in the Rhino.fi Console and through the [history API](/api-integration/status-history#list-your-transaction-history)

The Enhanced Compliance & Risk Management extension adds controls for clients with stricter compliance requirements.

## What's included

### Custom AML screening rules

Add your own compliance provider on top of Rhino.fi's screening. You provide rules via the API and can use any third-party service, for example [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/), [Elliptic](https://www.elliptic.co/) or your own in-house solution.

This lets you apply custom risk score thresholds, jurisdiction-based blocking rules, or extra checks against third-party sanctions lists, tailored to your regulatory environment and risk appetite.

Contact your account manager to discuss configuration options.

### Real-time AML notifications

When a deposit triggers a compliance flag, a webhook event is sent in real time. Your system can react immediately, for example by holding the transaction, alerting your compliance team or starting a review workflow, without polling the status endpoint.

AML notification events arrive in your standard webhook stream. See the [events list](/webhook/events-list) for the event types and payloads.

### Slack notifications (optional add-on)

Rhino.fi can send Slack notifications to your team when an end customer's deposit fails KYT screening. This gives you immediate visibility of compliance events without a webhook integration. Contact your account manager to set this up.
