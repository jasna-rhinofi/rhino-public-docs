**Extension tier:** Premium

Rhino.fi supports a wide range of chains and a standard set of stablecoins out of the box. See [Supported chains and tokens](/get-started/supported-chains). The Custom Chain & Token Support extension is for clients whose users hold assets on chains, or in tokens, that aren't in the standard set.

## What can be added

- **New EVM chains:** any EVM-compatible chain where Rhino.fi can deploy liquidity and run a solver.
- **New non-EVM chains:** evaluated case by case, depending on the chain's tooling and available liquidity.
- **Additional tokens:** stablecoins or other fungible tokens not yet returned by the [configs endpoint](/api-reference/bridge/configs/configs-chains-&-tokens).

## How to request

Custom chain and token additions are not self-serve. Rhino.fi's engineering team evaluates each request based on:

- Liquidity depth on the target chain
- Security and smart contract auditability
- Operational overhead and monitoring requirements
- Client volume commitment

To request a new chain or token, contact your account manager with the chain or token details and your expected volume.

## Timelines

Evaluating and onboarding a new EVM chain typically takes 1–2 days from agreement. Non-EVM chains vary, depending on tooling and liquidity.

Adding an established stablecoin on an already-supported chain is usually faster.
