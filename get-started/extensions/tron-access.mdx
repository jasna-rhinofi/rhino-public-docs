**Extension tier:** Premium

Tron, Solana and Bitcoin all have higher onchain transaction costs than other supported chains, so access to them is enabled through this extension. 

## What's enabled

| Feature | What it does | Cost per transaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tron Smart Deposit Addresses** | Create SDAs on Tron. Users send [supported tokens](/get-started/supported-chains) to the SDA, and funds settle to your configured destination. | \$1.40 |
| **Tron as a destination** | Bridge transfers can settle to a Tron address. | \$1.40 |
| **Solana as a destination** | Bridge transfers can settle to a Solana address. | \$0.50 |
| **Bitcoin Smart Deposit Addresses** | Create SDAs on Bitcoin. Users send BTC to the SDA, and funds settle to your configured destination. See [Interacting with Bitcoin](/interacting-with-bitcoin). | \$1 |

## Who pays

For each of these chains, you choose who pays the additional cost:

- **You.** The cost appears in the sponsored section of the quote and is invoiced at month end.
- **Your end customer.** The cost appears in the non-sponsored section of the quote and is deducted from the amount settled on the destination chain.

Your account manager sets this up with you. See [Fees](/get-started/fees-and-limits#onchain-costs).

## Why this is a separate extension

These chains need specific infrastructure, compliance controls and operational processes that differ from EVM chains, and they cost more per transaction. 

To enable this extension, contact your Rhino.fi account manager. 

## Integration

Once the extension is enabled on your account, Tron and Solana appear in the [configs endpoint](/api-reference/bridge/configs/configs-chains-&-tokens) (`GET /bridge/configs`) as available chains, with `enabledDepositAddress: true`. No code changes are needed beyond checking that these chains are listed.

- **SDAs on Tron or Bitcoin:** use `"TRON"` or `"BITCOIN"` in `depositChains`. These need their own create request: one request can cover several EVM chains, but Tron, Solana and Bitcoin SDAs are each created separately.
- **Tron as a destination:** use `"TRON"` as `chainOut` for bridge transfers, or `destinationChain` for SDAs.
- **Solana as a destination:** use `"SOLANA"` in the same way.

See [Smart Deposit Addresses](/api-integration/smart-deposits) and [Bridge transfers](/api-integration/bridge) for the full flows.
