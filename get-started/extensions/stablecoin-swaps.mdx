title: "1:1 Stablecoin Settlement"
description: "USDT↔USDC conversion at a guaranteed 1:1 rate, no slippage, no market exposure."

**Extension tier:** Premium

When the 1:1 Stablecoin Settlement extension is enabled, conversions between USDT and USDC run at a guaranteed 1:1 rate, within the guardrails and limits below. There is no slippage, no market exposure and no difference between the deposited amount and the settled amount, net of fees.

## The problem this solves

Without this extension, a cross-stable swap (for example, a user deposits USDT and the destination expects USDC) is routed through a market. The settled amount depends on the USDT/USDC price at the time of execution, and in thin markets it can move noticeably away from 1:1.

For applications that show a settlement amount to end users, such as onboarding flows, payment screens or balance estimates, any difference creates a poor experience and extra support requests.

## How it works

Rhino.fi holds its own USDT and USDC reserves on supported chains. When a cross-stable deposit arrives, Rhino.fi credits the destination from its reserves at exactly 1:1, then rebalances its own positions separately. For your user, the USDT amount deposited equals the USDC amount settled, minus fees.

This works in both directions, USDT → USDC and USDC → USDT, on every supported chain where both tokens are available.

### Guardrails

Rhino.fi provides 1:1 settlement while the USDT/USDC market rate stays within 3 basis points of 1:1. This is the Rhino.fi guardrail.

With the [Advanced Fee & Limit Management](/get-started/extensions/advanced-fee-management) extension, you can set your own wider guardrail, and Rhino.fi continues to settle at 1:1 up to it. Beyond your guardrail, settlement returns to the market rate.

## Integration

No API change is needed. If your account has this extension enabled, any SDA or bridge request with a cross-stable `tokenOut` automatically uses 1:1 pricing. See [Smart Deposit Addresses](/api-integration/smart-deposits#settle-in-a-different-token) and [Bridge transfers](/api-integration/bridge#swap-tokens-during-a-transfer).

To confirm the extension is active on your account, contact your account manager. With Advanced Fee & Limit Management, you can also see which of your projects have 1:1 enabled in the Rhino.fi Console.

## Limits

1:1 settlement follows the same deposit limits as standard SDA deposits, plus account-level and recipient-level volume limits that protect your app from abuse. When a limit is reached, settlement falls back to the market rate, and if only one recipient reaches their limit, only that recipient's transactions fall back. For transactions above \$1M USD equivalent, contact your account manager to confirm available liquidity.

See [Limits](/get-started/limits) for the details.
