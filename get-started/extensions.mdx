---
title: "Extensions"
description: "Extensions are premium features that expand the capabilities of the Rhino.fi Stablecoin Activation Stack. They are activated at the account level: your account manager enables them when you sign up, or later as an upgrade."
---

Extensions are designed for teams with specific requirements: custom compliance workflows, programmable settlement outcomes, custom chain coverage or advanced fee management.

<Note>
  Extensions are available on subscription plans. They are not available on trial accounts. See [Trial accounts: what's included and how it differs from a subscription](https://learn.rhino.fi/articles/8966431786-trial-accounts-what-s-included-and-how-it-differs-from-a-subscription).
</Note>

## Available extensions

| Extension | Tier | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Enhanced Compliance & Risk Management](/get-started/extensions/enhanced-compliance) | Free + Premium | Custom AML screening rules and real-time AML event notifications |
| [Automated Onchain Actions](/get-started/extensions/automated-onchain-actions) | Premium | Execute a contract call automatically when settled funds arrive on the destination chain. Set with the `postBridgeData` field in the API. |
| [1:1 Stablecoin Settlement](/get-started/extensions/stablecoin-swaps) | Premium | USDT↔USDC conversion at a guaranteed 1:1 rate, with no slippage |
| [Advanced Fee & Limit Management](/get-started/extensions/advanced-fee-management) | Free + Premium | Set client surcharges per customer or route, and view your limits in the Console |
| [Custom Chain & Token Support](/get-started/extensions/custom-chain-token-support) | Premium | Expand supported chains or tokens beyond the standard set |
| [Tron, Solana and Bitcoin Access](/get-started/extensions/tron-access) | Premium | Enable transactions on Tron, Solana and Bitcoin, which have additional onchain costs. You choose whether you or your end customer pays them. |

## Enabling extensions

Extensions are not self-serve. To enable any extension, contact your account manager.

All extensions are governed by your master service agreement. Some extensions have additional contractual requirements. For example, Enhanced Compliance & Risk Management requires a data processing addendum.
